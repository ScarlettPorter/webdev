My Top Three Favorite Music Artists

These are a few music artists I could listen to on repeat without ever getting bored of them.

Top Three

  1. The Antlers
  2. As Tall As Lions
  3. Artificial Language

Why I Love These Artists

  • The Antlers music is very atmospheric and resonates with the listeners emotions.
  • Even though As Tall As Lions has disbanded, their work was a mix of indie rock and some jazz influences that make their music very melodic.
  • Artificial Language has a mix of metal music and unique choruses, which really vibes with my spirit.
